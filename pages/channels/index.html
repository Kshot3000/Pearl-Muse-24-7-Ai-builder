Open a channel

Lock PRL into a 2-of-2 Taproot output shared with one counterparty. Pearl has no smart contracts, so the channel is pure script: both of you must sign to move the funds — on-chain via a cooperative close, or off-chain by exchanging signed commitment transactions that either of you can broadcast. Your money, your keys, no custodian.

Channel state

    
Each payment is a new state: both balances, a fresh pair of commitment
    transactions, and revoked old secrets. Propose a payment, sign your copies, exchange with your peer
    (export / import below), then revoke the old state. The latest fully-signed state is the truth.

Revocation

      
Activating a new state revokes the old one. Reveal your old revocation secret to your
      peer — with it, they can penalize you if you ever broadcast the revoked state. Record the secret
      they reveal to you.

Close the channel

    
Two ways out. Cooperative needs both signatures but settles immediately.
    Unilateral needs only you: broadcast your latest fully-signed commitment, wait out the
    CSV delay, then claim your output. Never broadcast an old state — your peer holds the revocation secret.

    

      

        
Cooperative close

        
Immediate outputs to both payout addresses. Fee split equally.

Verify

    
Standalone verifier. Paste a channel descriptor and any signed channel transaction —
    every signature is re-checked, outputs are matched against the expected state, and the verdict is
    PROVEN or NOT PROVEN with reasons. No keys needed.

      Honest limits
      
    
        
      
      • No routing, no multi-hop, no invoices — one channel, one peer, fixed capacity set at open.
        • 
      
      • State exchange is manual: export a bundle, send it to your peer however you like, import theirs.
        • 
      
      • This page is not a watchtower. If you are offline, your peer can broadcast a revoked state — use Pearl Watch for monitoring.
        • 
      
      • The penalty model is opt-in training wheels: revocation secrets are exchanged by hand, and the claim delay is in blocks, not wall-clock time.
        • 
      
      • Commitment fees are fixed when a state is signed; if chain fees spike, a unilateral close can get expensive.
        • 
      
      • Broadcasting a revoked state lets your peer take your entire to_local output. Don't.
