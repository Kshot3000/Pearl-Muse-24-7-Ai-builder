Honest limits

    
    
      
  • Sealing costs real PRL on a live chain. A notarization is two
      on-chain transactions (commit, then reveal) — it needs a funded address and a
      reachable Blockbook endpoint. Nothing here broadcasts without your click, and
      nothing here can seal without funds.
    • 
      
  • The chain is the witness, not a lawyer. This is
      proof-of-existence timestamping — it proves a document existed at a time, not
      who wrote it or whether it is true. Nothing here is legal advice.
    • 
      
  • Your document never leaves this machine. Only its SHA-256
      fingerprint is engraved on-chain; the file or text you drop here is hashed locally.
    • 
      
  • Confirmations matter. The reveal is only valid once the commit
      transaction confirms — revealing early burns the fee and seals nothing.
    • 
      
  • Keys live in this page's memory only. Generated or imported
      secrets are never sent anywhere; reloading the page forgets them. Back up any key
      that guards real funds.
