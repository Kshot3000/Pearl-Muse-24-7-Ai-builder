Honest limits — read before any real PRL moves

  • Two-player mode needs a live counterparty running this protocol. This page coordinates through shareable bundles, but it cannot force anyone to reveal a secret or co-sign a settle. A sore loser can stall until the timeout — then each side refunds its own deposit.
  • "Provably fair" means the outcome is fixed before you bet and verifiable after — the dealer's sha256(secret) commitment binds the result before funding. It does not mean the dealer can't get lucky.
  • Timeout is an absolute block height. After it passes, each depositor can unilaterally refund their own deposit — nobody can touch the other's coins. Until then, nobody can refund.
  • Solo practice is a protocol demo. No PRL moves, no funding, no broadcast — it shows the commit → reveal → verify flow against a local dealer.
  • Timeouts are capped at 52,560 blocks (~4 months at ~194s/block). The page shows a wall-clock estimate; the chain decides.

3 · Fund — each side stakes its own escrow

Each party funds their own escrow address with exactly their stake. The two addresses share the 2-of-2 settle leaf, but each binds its depositor — a deposit can only ever refund to its depositor or settle 2-of-2.

The dealer reveals the secret. The page checks it against the locked commitment, derives the outcome deterministically, and scores the winner. Anyone can re-verify this independently.

5 · Settle — the loser co-signs the winner's payout

The winner builds an unsigned settle bundle (both deposits in, winner paid out, exact fee). The loser imports it, re-verifies the outcome and the math independently, signs, and the winner finalizes + broadcasts after a double confirmation.

Loser: import, verify, sign
