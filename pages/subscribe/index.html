Pearl Subscribe

Standing orders for PRL — the chronometer desk

The service

Set up a recurring PRL payment — a salary, a retainer, a subscription. Pearl has no smart contracts, so this desk builds a consent structure: you fund one transaction with one output per period, then pre-sign each period's payment with a block-height locktime the network enforces. The merchant broadcasts each payment when it matures. You can cancel any not-yet-due period by spending its funding output back to yourself — the pre-signed payment can then never confirm.

Honest limits — read before you fund anything

  • Consent, not enforcement. Pre-signed transactions are your standing permission — the chain cannot force the merchant to broadcast, nor force you to keep funding.
  • The merchant holds the payments. You hand them signed transactions; they broadcast each one after its locktime. If they lose them, nothing is paid (your funds stay reclaimable).
  • Cancel = double-spend. Cancelling a period spends its funding output back to you. If the merchant's payment confirms first, that period is paid — there is a race, and the desk shows it honestly.
  • Locktimes are block heights. Period lengths are approximate (Pearl targets 194 s/block); the network enforces heights, not dates.
  • Test on testnet first. Keys are wiped after signing and never stored, but verify the whole flow with tPRL before real money.

The funding

One transaction with one output per period, each worth amount + fee reserve, all to your anchor address. Build it from your UTXOs (Blockbook scan or paste), sign it locally with your key — the page refuses any key that does not control the anchor address — then broadcast. The key is wiped the moment signing finishes.

The pre-signed payments

One payment per period, each spending its funding output: merchant gets the exact amount, dust-free change returns to your anchor, nLockTime is the period's height and sequence = 0xfffffffe so the network enforces it. Every signature is re-verified against its own BIP-341 digest before the hex is shown. Hand the signed bundle to the merchant — that is the standing order.

The ledger

Watch every period: upcoming, due (locktime passed — the merchant may broadcast), paid (spent by the exact pre-signed payment), cancelled (spent by anything else). Reads Blockbook GET-only; paste values manually if the endpoint is unreachable.

The escape hatch

Reclaim any period whose funding output is still unspent: spend it back to your anchor in a transaction with no locktime. Once that confirms, the pre-signed payment for that period can never confirm (its input is gone). The race is real — if the merchant already broadcast the payment and it confirms first, the period is paid. Check the ledger first.

The verifier

Paste any pearl-sub-bundle:v1 signed bundle — yours or the merchant's. The page re-validates the descriptor fingerprint, recomputes every payment txid from the funding plan, checks each signature against its own BIP-341 digest, and confirms amounts, change, locktimes, and sequences. Anything off is reported loudly, per period.
