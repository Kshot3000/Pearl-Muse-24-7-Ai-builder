Honest limits — read before any real PRL moves

  • "Goal met" is a social rule, not a cryptographic one. The release leaf is spendable the moment the recipient and the creator both sign — even before the goal is reached, even minutes after launch. This page's Release tab refuses to build below the goal, but nothing on-chain enforces that.
  • The creator can refuse after the goal is met. Release needs the creator's signature too. If the creator withholds it, funds stay locked until the deadline — then every backer can refund unilaterally.
  • Every backer keeps a unilateral post-deadline refund. After the tide turns (chain height ≥ deadline), any backer can take their pledge back alone with their own key. Nobody can stop a refund, and nobody needs permission.
  • Descriptors have no checksum. A single flipped character can silently describe a different campaign. Always pair a descriptor with its fingerprint (shown on every card) — the verifier refuses loudly on any mismatch.
  • Needs live Blockbook + real funds for the real thing. Funding scans, chain height, fee estimates and broadcast all go through the Blockbook endpoint you configure. No real campaigns were funded in testing — the cryptography is real; the mainnet miles are not.
  • Secrets stay in this page. Keys you paste are kept in page memory only and wiped with the lock button. Verify every address and amount out of band before funding.
