Pearl Vesting

time-locked vesting schedules for PRL

Forge the schedule

A funder locks PRL for a beneficiary in N timelocked tranches on Taproot. Each tranche is its own address, spendable by the beneficiary only after its unlock time (<lock> OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY). Optionally revocable: the funder can reclaim still-unclaimed tranches after they mature. Keys and secrets never leave this page.

Advanced: custom unlock schedule (one YYYY-MM-DD, PRL per line)

When set, this overrides cliff / vest period / tranche count. Locks are midnight UTC of each date.

Tranches — fund each address

Send exactly the listed amount to each tranche address. The funder's wallet pays these as ordinary PRL sends. Nothing is locked until funded.

Schedule descriptor

Hand this to the beneficiary (or save it). Anyone can re-derive every address from it and verify the funder funded the right ones — it is tamper-evident.

Track the schedule

Paste a schedule descriptor to re-derive every tranche address, then scan funding and maturity against Blockbook.

Claim matured tranches

The beneficiary imports their secret (mnemonic or WIF — never stored, never sent anywhere), picks a matured funded tranche, and builds a locally-signed claim transaction. One transaction per tranche, exact fee math, signature re-verified before broadcast.

Honest limits

    
    
      
  • Nothing is locked until the funder actually sends PRL to each tranche address — the schedule is a plan until funded.
    • 
      
  • Maturity is read from Blockbook's latest block time (or your clock, labeled when it is). A claim broadcast before maturity will be rejected by the network.
    • 
      
  • Locktimes are absolute: a tranche set to unlock on a date unlocks regardless of price or anything else.
    • 
      
  • Test on testnet first. This is a timelocking tool, not legal or tax advice.
